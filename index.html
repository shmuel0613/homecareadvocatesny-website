Questions

Things families ask us

What is OPWDD?

OPWDD is the New York State Office for People With Developmental Disabilities. It oversees supports and services for people with developmental disabilities, including autism. We can help you understand your options and connect with providers.

Does it cost anything to use your service?

No. Our help is free for families. Participating home care agencies and ABA providers pay us. Any services you later receive are arranged with the provider, who can explain coverage and costs.

Which areas do you serve?

Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Manhattan.

What happens after I send my information?

We reach out by phone or text to learn more about what you need, then introduce you to providers that serve your area. You decide who to speak with and whether to move forward.

Can you tell me if my child qualifies?

No. Eligibility decisions are made by the agencies and programs that run each service. We can point you to the right place to ask.

How is my information used?

We use it to contact you and to introduce you to participating providers. You can read the details in our privacy policy, and you can opt out of calls and texts at any time.
