Home Care Advocates NY

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Home Care Advocates NY (“we,” “us”) is an advertising and referral service. Participating home care agencies and applied behavior analysis (ABA) providers pay us for advertising and intake services. We are not a healthcare provider and we do not give medical advice. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website or our lead form, and what we do with it.

Who we are

This website and our Facebook and Instagram pages are operated by Home Care Advocates NY. You can reach us by email at afdv0613@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Anything you write in the note box, such as what kind of help you are looking for. Please do not include Social Security numbers, insurance numbers or detailed medical records.
  • The ad or page you responded to, the date and time, and basic device and browser information.
  • Recordings or transcripts of calls and texts with our automated assistant and our staff, so we can follow up and check quality.

We collect information from parents, guardians and other adults. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the help you asked about.
  • To confirm that your inquiry is a genuine request for services in the New York City area.
  • To connect you with participating home care agencies or ABA providers that may be able to help.
  • To run and improve our advertising, website and follow-up.
  • To meet legal obligations and keep our systems secure.

Calls and text messages

When you submit our form, you agree to be contacted at the number you gave. We use this consent statement on our forms:

By submitting this form, I agree that Home Care Advocates NY and participating home care agencies and ABA providers may call or text me at the number provided, including with automated technology, about home care and related services. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or tell us on any call.

We honor opt-out requests promptly. Your phone number is not shared for third-party marketing unrelated to your request.

Who we share it with

  • Participating providers. Home care agencies and ABA providers that serve your area receive your name, contact details and note so they can contact you. Once you speak with a provider, that provider’s own privacy practices apply to what you tell them.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us run our website, forms, calling and texting, customer records and email, who may use the information only to serve us.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, or to protect people or our rights.

We do not sell your information to data brokers.

Cookies and advertising tools

Our website and ads use cookies and tools such as the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure how our ads perform and to show our ads to people who may be interested. You can limit ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings and block cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to follow up and to meet legal and business record requirements, then delete or de-identify it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, such as access controls and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • Stop calls and texts at any time by replying STOP or telling us.
  • Ask us to stop sharing your information with providers we have not yet sent it to.

Email afdv0613@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.

Home Care Advocates NY is an advertising and referral service paid by participating home care agencies and ABA providers. We are not a healthcare provider.
